## Why this exists

System 06 works and is now frozen: +25.71% on the sealed year, a worst year of -2.96%
against BTC's -72.77%, and it beats every trivial baseline on the one year nobody could
fit. It also does not meet the mandate of +30% every calendar year, and three weeks of
searching produced three sealed readings, all lost.

The reason the third was lost is the reason this initiative exists. `min_hold` 32
replicated on seven of eight walk-forward boundaries with a median advantage of +2% and a
year-to-year spread from 0.76x to 1.52x — it wins about seven years in ten. One sealed
year cannot settle a seven-in-ten coin, and nobody had computed those odds before
spending the reading.

So the answer is not another lever on system 06. It is a laboratory where the next system
cannot repeat those three mistakes, and where work already paid for is available instead
of re-done.

## What it covers

1. **One shared data catalogue.** Every series this laboratory has downloaded, behind a
   single import, with the 2026 lock structural rather than conventional, and an
   inventory that names what is MISSING as loudly as what is present.
2. **A documentation standard with teeth.** Each system carries the compressed record of
   what it tried and above all what it REFUSED — the part that cannot be reconstructed
   from code. Enforced by tests; one box per system on the public page.
3. **System 08, opened blank.** No hypothesis until the operator sets one. Its working
   process is code: seven ordered stages, and the sealed window has one door with three
   locks — the third being an edge whose worst measured year still wins.

## How it will be judged

System 08 beats the number on the table — +25.71% sealed, at a drawdown reported beside
it — having paid for that reading with evidence the gate accepted. Anything else is a
refusal, written down with the same keystrokes as a win.
