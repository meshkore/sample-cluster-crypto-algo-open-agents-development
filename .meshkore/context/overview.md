Public, autonomous crypto quantitative research laboratory. It continuously
generates numbered long-only strategies, backtests them strictly before
2026, and forward-tests only promoted candidates from 1 January 2026 through
today. The live monitor exposes the current experiment and best forward result.
The system is research-only and never places live orders.
