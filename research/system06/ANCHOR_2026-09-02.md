## What the system is (the techniques)

1. **Oracle labels** (`oracle.py`): 3% zigzag decomposition with perfect hindsight over
   2018–2025; the network learns to imitate the oracle and never sees 2026.
2. **Network**: causal TCN, 96-bar window of 15m candles, **192×192×192 channels**
   (255,937 parameters — the P34/P35 discovery: the 64-channel net had been underfitting
   from the start), dropout 0.1, 50 epochs, lr 1e-3, uniqueness weighting 1, 44 causal
   features, 14 pooled symbols (universe `universe.json:14`).
3. **Seed 91002** — the anti-optimism rule: the **lower-middle** of P35's four verified
   seeds (+0.6008 / +0.4983 / **+0.4238** / +0.3857), committed in
   `tools/champion_192.py` BEFORE measuring. The headline is never built on the luckiest
   draw.
4. **Risk layer** (exact values live in `best.json` → `band` + `risk`): hysteresis enter
   0.75 / exit 0.25 / min_hold 16; at most 2 positions at 15%; stop 8%, trailing 12%;
   breadth_gate 0.3; regime_deploy 0.5; meta-labelling (margin 0.005); money model 0.5;
   extreme-fear veto at F&G < 25; drawdown mandate 50%.
5. **Execution realism** (2021 audit, `tools/audit_2021.py`): minimum $100 per order,
   maximum 10% of the candle's traded value (`min_notional`, `max_participation` in
   `orchestrator.py`, test `test_execution_realism.py`). 2021 deflates from ×1220 to ×199.
   On top of that, **market impact in the shared engine** (`CostModel.impact_bps = 60`,
   square-root law, charged on exits too — a stop fires in exactly the thinnest candle):
   2021 lands at ×146 and 2026 at +25.71%. The scale of the problem: ACEUSDT's median 15m
   candle trades $17,942, so a $15,000 entry was 84% of the candle and paid a flat 5 bps.

## The discipline that makes these numbers trustworthy

- **2026 is sealed**: read at the end, once, with the selection already committed.
- **Reproducible bar +0.4611** = the median of P35's four seeds. A future candidate must
  beat THAT in research years before it earns a look at 2026.
- **Forensic 2021 audit**: the ledger reconstructs equity to the cent; cash never negative;
  real prices within candles; zero phantom fills (`rnd/audit_2021_*.json`).
- **The trade ledger** (`attribution.py`): 2026 captures 2.2% of what was available — the
  improvement headroom is in capturing more, not in betting harder.

## From here (the operator's directions)

Consistency, safety, minimise drawdown **without neutering the upside**. The ledger says
where: entries with nothing behind them (~1/3) are concentrated in one corner of the
indicator space, and 97% of the opportunities go uncaptured. A73 (per-indicator response
curves) is the instrument for turning that into levers, always through walk-forward plus a
paired A/B. Nothing is adopted against 2026.
