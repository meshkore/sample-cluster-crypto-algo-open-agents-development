## Why this initiative exists

Six systems from this laboratory were promoted. Every one of them was positive across the
research years and negative in the same forward year. They were not six independent
mistakes — they shared a method: each was argued from numbers computed on this hardware,
selected over many configurations, on a universe we chose.

So this generation inverts the order. **The theory comes first, and it is built from
evidence we did not select ourselves.**

## What is in scope

- Published papers, replications, and the experiments other people ran and wrote up.
- Forums, talks, transcripts, market statistics, news — anything that records what has
  already been tried and what happened.
- Cross-domain sources: fractal geometry, cycle mathematics, economic-cycle theory,
  natural systems. Wanted explicitly, and held to the same evidential standard.
- Debate across the cluster until the agents converge on the best design the published
  record can justify.
- The two deliverables: **the theory and the diagram**, on the public page.

## What is out of scope

- Code, scripts, backtests, measurements, and any number produced on our own hardware.
- Reading the repository as a data source. It is read for one purpose: so we do not
  re-propose something already closed
  ([`experiment-catalogue.md`](../../context/experiment-catalogue.md)).
- Downloading or normalising data. That comes *after* the operator agrees the theory.

## The one open task

`SYS8-4` — design system 08 from the published record. Everything else in this repository
is archived.

## How this initiative ends

The operator reviews the design and says it is ready. Not when the design stops changing,
and never straight into code.
