## What the evidence actually says

Three findings, each measured more than once:

1. **The thin years are thin in the price.** At 3% legs with real costs, 2022, 2023 and
   2025 do not contain enough up-moves to clear costs — and the 1h grid shows the same
   drought, so it is not an artefact of how we slice time.
2. **The book is ~98% in cash.** It returns roughly 500% on the capital it actually
   risks; the account barely notices because the risk taken is tiny. Every attempt to
   deploy more by accepting worse trades has lost.
3. **The all-years-green record belongs to the shipped net, not to the method.**
   Reseeding the same recipe puts one seed in four negative, under both the old and the
   new configuration.

## What is left, honestly

Not tuning. Three structural directions remain:

- **New markets or instruments** — the material this book does not touch. The measured
  fact that the drought is in the price rather than the method points here first.
- **New information** — the one seam that produced a win. Funding is parked (weak),
  on-chain is refuted, but the seam itself is proven and barely explored.
- **Accept lean years** — a book that earns 20–40% in good years and near zero in lean
  ones is what the evidence describes. Whether that is acceptable is the operator's
  call, not a technical question.
