## Scope

Maintain an auditable liquid operational universe and causal historical
capacity constraints. Replace binary-entry confidence with a continuously
scored, pre-2026-trained candidate only after independent review specifies its
labels, walk-forward protocol and falsification tests.

## Current increment

The liquid universe records public 24-hour turnover and trade count, restricted
to the top qualifying 100 assets. Historical entries require trailing dollar
volume and cannot consume more than the configured participation rate. The
candidate model remains deliberately pending; a neural network is not admitted
without a strictly pre-2026 training and validation protocol.
