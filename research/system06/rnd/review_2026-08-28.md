# Strategic review — where each piece stands, and where its maximum is
*2026-08-28 · written on the operator's request to re-examine the whole research loop
with fresh eyes. Every claim below is a measurement from this repository, not an opinion.*

## The production chain, and where the constraint actually sits

Money is produced by a chain: **generate** candidates (the net + its labels) → **filter**
them (meta, breadth, trend) → **size** them (deploy ceiling, money model, taper) →
**survive** (drawdown handling). Two weeks of measurements now locate the constraint at
each link:

| link | state | evidence | remaining upside |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sizing / money mgmt** | **near its measured maximum** | ceiling 0.50 = +0.0308 (best result of the project); money_model = +0.023 (1.8 SE); dd_sizer built; abort removed by operator | P11/P12 curve + banking money_model: perhaps +0.03–0.06 more, paid for in drawdown |
| **Filtering** | **binding constraint in thin years** | meta survival: 60.6% (2020) → 1.3% (2022) → 0.5% (2025); 29,653 candidates → 155 | P13 running. BUT the raw rule earns NO gross profit in 2022/23/25 — the filter may be *correctly* refusing garbage. If P13 loses, this link is also at its max |
| **Generation** | **the deep constraint, least developed** | raw candidates lose money in 3 of the last 4 research years; two independent disciplines both inverted in the sealed window; the search sits 3.6σ from its own trigger | **This is where "train better models" lives** — see below |
| **Risk / survival** | resolved | abort never bound at current size (P10 all-inert); taper (dd_sizer) replaces the cliff that forfeited 2021 | execution detail now, not research |

**The honest arithmetic of "more money every year":** filter + sizing tuning can
plausibly move 2022–2025 from ~+6%/yr to +10–15%/yr at more drawdown. Multiplying
beyond that requires candidates that *earn* in flat years — a generation problem.
No amount of sizing multiplies a year whose 34 trades turn negative without their
single best one.

## The specific hole in generation, found today

`FEATURE_COLUMNS` (44 features) is **entirely per-symbol**. The net sees its own coin's
trend, volatility, candles and flow — and **nothing about the market it trades in**: no
breadth, no BTC state, no cross-sectional rank, no market-wide volatility. Every
market-level judgment lives in *post-hoc filters* the net cannot learn from.

This coheres with three prior measurements: the horse-race lever (cross-asset lead-lag
as a *bolt-on*) was null; the meta filter (which *does* see outcomes across the market,
implicitly) is worth more than every sizing overlay combined; and the net's edge
collapses precisely in years whose character is market-wide (2022 bear, 2025 chop).
The cheapest generation upgrade is therefore **market-state features** (A59): append to
each symbol's matrix the same causal market columns — breadth, BTC trend/return,
median cross-sectional volatility, the symbol's momentum rank. Paired test: same seed,
features on/off. Regime-conditional labels (A53) follow if A59 moves the needle;
architecture bake-offs (A07/A35/A36) only after, since they are the expensive way to
learn the same thing.

## The autoloop: reoriented, not stopped

The genome search cannot out-draw its own champion (3.6σ; ~1 in 150 historically). Its
real value today is (a) supplying fresh nets on which the 13-row grid A/Bs every lever
per iteration, and (b) hosting seed-verified promotion when a candidate does clear. That
is worth keeping — but the *findings* come from the paired-experiment runner, and the
program queue is the steering wheel. The daily paper harvest is now tied to the current
binding constraint (market-state features, regime-conditional training) rather than
generic reading.
