# Rocket — the idea cocktail

Each family is generated by `rocket.py` (functions `fam_*`), tried round-robin, so every kind of
idea is tried from the first hour. Status and results: `REPORT.md`.

| # | family | what | size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ma_scan | price vs its simple MA, every n = 2..300, on 1d / 4h / 1h of the coin; on 1d / 4h of BTC; and the share of all coins below their MA (the operator's "70% of the market below an average") | 1,993 |
| 2 | rsi | RSI n = 2..60 and 70..300, coin and BTC, 1d / 4h / 1h | 498 |
| 3 | macd | MACD histogram, fast 3–19, slow 13–89, signal 5/9/13, coin / BTC / market share, 1d / 4h / 1h | ~700 |

| 5 | indicators | EMA distance, MA slope, rate of change, volatility, vol ratio, z-score, drawdown, range position, variance ratio (trend vs mean reversion), skew, kurtosis, autocorrelation, efficiency ratio × 28 periods × 3 timeframes, coin and BTC | 2,184 |
| 6 | cross_section | the coin relative to BTC, its rank among the coins, the market mean, the market dispersion, the share below zero | ~1,600 |
| 7 | data / macro / funding / daily | other markets (VIX level and change, Nasdaq 20/60 d, dollar, oil, 10-year, yield curve), funding 1–63 settlements, fear & greed, BTC open interest, stablecoin supply, ETF flows (levels, averages, changes, all lagged ≥ 2 days), breadth | ~45 |

Ideas to add by hand as they come (operator or me): write them here, then add a generator.
