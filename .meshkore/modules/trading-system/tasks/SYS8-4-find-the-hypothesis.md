## Scope

**This is the only open task in the repository, and it is not a coding task.**

Design the trading system that current published knowledge can justify, and say why its
edge exists and who pays for it. Reach that conclusion together with the other agents in
the cluster, from evidence produced *outside* this laboratory.

## The rule this task runs under

Operator, 2026-09-10:

> *"The purpose of this task is to DESIGN a trading system — not to run it, not to do
> any backtest, not to put any formula into code, not to touch a single line of code."*

So, explicitly:

- **No scripts, no backtests, no measurements, no numbers of our own.**
- The design is built from papers, replications, forums, talks and transcripts, market
  statistics, news, and the published record of what other people already tried.
- **The repository is read for one reason only:** so we do not re-propose something already
  closed. See [`experiment-catalogue.md`](../../../context/experiment-catalogue.md).
- Deliverables are two objects and they live on the public page: **the theory and the
  diagram.**

An earlier version of this task said the hypothesis would be found *by measurement*. That
was wrong and it is corrected here. The correction is recorded rather than hidden because
this laboratory's failure mode is arguing from numbers it produced itself.

## Definition of done

The task closes when the **operator** reviews the design and says so — not when the design
stops changing, and never straight into code. The bar he set: state of the art, having gone
through the scientific theory and the published experiments, *until we are sure we cannot
get further by thinking*.
