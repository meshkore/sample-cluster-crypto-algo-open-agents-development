Deviations from §2.1 (for the reviewer to accept or reject):
1. Capped at the top 14 to isolate survivorship from width (uncapped: up to 57 coins).
2. Turnover = Σ close × volume on 15m bars, not the venue's quote volume.
3. Each year's candidate list complete to ~rank 30 (only the screen's top 30 downloaded).
4. Models for year N train only on N's coin set (fixed at end N−1, used on training years ≤ N−2:
   mild hindsight inside training, none on N).
5. A coin delisted while held is marked at its last print and closed by the time cap.
6. Renames (MATIC→POL, FTM→S) treated as delist + new listing.
7. Breadth rebuilt from candles exactly as 06 does; equals 06's cache on all 3,005,366 bars of
   today's 14.
